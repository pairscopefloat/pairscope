How the score works

  • Momentum, up to 40: 2026 return of 100%+ scores 40, 50%+ scores 30, 20%+ scores 20, any gain 10. Crypto, baskets and stocks without return data get 10.
  • Open lane, up to 30: no coins scores 30, 1–2 coins 20, 3–9 coins 10.
  • In the news, up to 20: 10 or more headlines about the asset in the last 24 hours scores 20, 3 or more scores 10.
  • Proven demand, 10: a coin on this asset has reached $50K.
  • Transfer fee: 1% costs 10 points, 0.2% costs 3, because it eats holder rewards.

Reading the coins tab

  • Activity: Hot means 24h volume is at least the coin's market cap, Active means at least a quarter of it, Slow means some trading, Quiet means none today.
  • Paid to holders 7d: the paired-stock rewards Float sent to the coin's holders over the last week, in US dollars.
  • Graduating: a coin moves to a locked Raydium pool at about $150K. Coins near the end of the meter are close.

About the data

Coins, pairings, holders and volume refresh every 12 minutes. Stock returns and headlines refresh every hour. Coin counts and coin stats come from Float's public tokens page and its stats feed, stock returns from Yahoo Finance with Stooq as a backup, and headlines from Google News. Coin counts are grouped by ticker, so a stock offered by two issuers shows one combined count. If a source fails, the last good value is kept and marked as stale.

Pairscope is an independent tool and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Float. Not financial advice. Most launched coins lose value, and a strong stock can reverse quickly.
